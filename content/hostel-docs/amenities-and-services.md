# Amenities & Services FAQ

## Wi-Fi
Wi-Fi is available in all rooms and common areas, included in the base rent. If your connection is slow, raise a complaint under the "Wi-Fi" category and a technician will check the router within 24 hours.

## Laundry
Laundry service runs twice a week (Tuesday and Saturday). Clothes must be handed in by 9:00 AM on service days. Self-service washing machines are also available in the laundry room from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM.

## Housekeeping
Rooms are cleaned twice a week by housekeeping staff. Common areas are cleaned daily. Tenants can request an extra cleaning slot through the complaints section under the "Cleaning" category.

## Food Menu
The weekly food menu is posted in the app under the Food section every Sunday evening. Special dietary requests (vegetarian, no-onion-garlic, etc.) can be raised with the property manager and are accommodated where possible.

## Parking
Two-wheeler parking is available on a first-come basis and is free for residents. Four-wheeler parking is subject to availability and may carry an additional monthly charge depending on the property.

## Maintenance Requests
Any plumbing, electrical, or general maintenance issue should be logged as a complaint in the app with the relevant category and priority. Urgent issues (no water, no power, safety hazards) are marked "urgent" priority and are typically addressed within a few hours.
