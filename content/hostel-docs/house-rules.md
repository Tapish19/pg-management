# House Rules & General Policies

## Entry Timings
Tenants must be back inside the property by 11:00 PM on weekdays and 12:30 AM on weekends. Late entry after these hours requires prior written approval from the property manager, logged at least 4 hours in advance.

## Visitors
Visitors are allowed in common areas only, between 10:00 AM and 8:00 PM. Overnight guests are not permitted under any circumstances. All visitors must sign in at the front desk and carry a valid photo ID.

## Notice Period
Tenants must give a minimum of 30 days' written notice before vacating. The security deposit refund is processed within 7 working days after checkout, subject to a room inspection and deduction of any pending dues or damages.

## Room Changes
Room or bed reassignment requests must be submitted at least 15 days in advance and are subject to availability. A one-time room-change fee of ₹500 applies after the first free change.

## Prohibited Items
Smoking, alcohol, and open flames (candles, incense with open fire) are not permitted inside rooms. Cooking appliances other than an electric kettle are not allowed in private rooms; the shared kitchen may be used during designated hours.

## Maintenance of Common Areas
Tenants are expected to keep common areas (kitchen, lounge, laundry room) clean after use. Repeated violations may result in a cleaning fee being added to the monthly rent.
