# Payments, Rent & Fees

## Rent Due Date
Monthly rent is due on the 5th of every month. Payments made after the 5th are considered late and may attract a late fee of ₹100 per day, up to a maximum of ₹1,000 per month.

## Accepted Payment Methods
Rent can be paid via UPI, bank transfer, or the in-app payment gateway (powered by Razorpay). Cash payments are discouraged but accepted at the front desk with a signed receipt.

## Security Deposit
The security deposit is typically equal to one month's rent and is fully refundable at checkout, minus any deductions for damages, unpaid dues, or an early-exit penalty if notice period requirements were not met.

## Food Charges
Food plans are billed separately from rent and are optional. Tenants can opt in or out of the monthly food plan by informing the property manager before the 25th of the preceding month.

## Utility Charges
Electricity beyond a fair-usage limit (as displayed on each floor's meter board) is billed at actual cost, split among room occupants. Water and Wi-Fi are included in the base rent.

## Refund Timeline
All refunds (deposit, overpayment, or plan cancellations) are processed within 7 working days to the original payment method.
